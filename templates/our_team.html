Our Team

At Voces Sin Fronteras, our strength comes from our people; passionate young educators who believe in equity, community, and the power of language to connect the world.

Each of our volunteers brings unique talents, cultures, and dreams to our classrooms, creating a learning experience that is dynamic, inclusive, and full of heart.

Meet the amazing team behind VSF

Kiara ViDemantay Villantay
Founder & Director

Hola a todos! My name is Kiara, and I am a dual citizen of Perú and the United States. I founded Voces Sin Fronteras during my junior year of high school because I wanted to give children in Hispanic countries the same access to English that others take for granted. I have been teaching English since I was a sophomore, and I love seeing our students grow in confidence each week.

My favorite Peruvian foods are mote, picarones, and ceviche!
