Our Story

Voces Sin Fronteras (VSF) began with a simple idea: that learning English could be a doorway to connection and equality.

Our founder, Kiara ViDemantay, wanted to create a program that gave children in Latin America access to quality English education, regardless of their financial situation. What started as a small online class in Peru has now grown into a community of over 70 students and 8 dedicated volunteer teachers, all united by the same purpose: to make learning accessible and meaningful.
