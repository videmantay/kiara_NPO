Our Classes

Our classes are designed to be fun, practical, and culturally rich.

Students from elementary, middle and high school learn to use English in real-life situations: talking about family, school, holidays, and hobbies ; while also discovering the diversity of American culture. Lessons are interactive, full of games, songs, and cultural themes like Halloween, Thanksgiving, or everyday life in the U.S.

Classes are taught entirely online and completely free for students. Our teachers are passionate high school volunteers who receive guidance and training to make each lesson engaging and supportive.

Our goal is to teach practical English that students can use right away; whether they're introducing themselves, describing their day, or talking about their favorite hobbies.

At VSF, learning English means more than grammar and vocabulary; it means opening a window to the world.
